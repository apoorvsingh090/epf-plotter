Ticker reference (yfinance)

Enter Yahoo Finance symbols, comma-separated. Examples:

  • US: AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL
  • India NSE: RELIANCE.NS, TCS.NS, INFY.NS
  • India BSE: RELIANCE.BO
  • Indices/FX/Crypto: ^GSPC, EURUSD=X, BTC-USD

Data is fetched daily (5y history). Symbols are case-insensitive.

CSV upload alternative: each file must have Date,Close columns. The filename (without extension) becomes the asset name.
